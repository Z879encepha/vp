North Edison. Between New York and Philadelphia.

Connected throughout the Northeast by rail, road, and air, with official resources for planning travel from the wider Edison region.

A wider regional story

Local convenience with access to major Northeast destinations.

Village Pointe is positioned in North Edison within Middlesex County. Edison and Metropark provide rail access, and Metropark is served by Amtrak with connections to New York City, Philadelphia, and Washington, D.C. Major roadways and airports expand the regional reach.

Travel time depends on route, schedule, traffic, weather, construction, and the specific starting point. Always check current official sources before leaving.
