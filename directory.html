Before hiring

Verification is part of the job.

  • Confirm identity, references, and recent work independently
  • Verify New Jersey licensing or registration when required
  • Request proof of insurance and a written estimate
  • Confirm Association approval and Edison permit requirements
  • Agree on scope, price, materials, warranty, cleanup, and payment terms
  • Protect keys, access codes, financial information, and identity documents
