Before hiring anyone

Verify first. Agree in writing.

  • Confirm identity, experience, references, and current availability
  • Verify New Jersey licensing or registration when the work requires it
  • Ask for insurance, written scope, price, materials, warranty, and cleanup terms
  • Confirm Association approval and Edison permits before regulated work begins
  • Do not provide keys, access codes, identity documents, or financial information unnecessarily
  • For emergencies or official property matters, use the appropriate official channel
