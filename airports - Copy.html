Airports & Regional Access

Seven airports. One exceptionally connected location.

North Edison sits within reach of major New York and Philadelphia airports, regional passenger alternatives, and one of the country's best-known business aviation gateways.

North Edison to the Region

Air access in almost every direction.

For residents and visitors, Newark is the natural primary airport. But North Edison also benefits from practical access to JFK, LaGuardia, Philadelphia, Trenton-Mercer, Teterboro and New York Stewart, creating an unusually broad set of aviation options within the surrounding region.

Airport Guide

The seven airports at a glance.

Airport roles differ considerably. Some are major commercial hubs, some offer more limited passenger service, and Teterboro primarily serves general and business aviation.

EWR
Approx. 13 direct miles

Newark Liberty International Airport

North Edison's primary major airport and the most convenient starting point for a wide range of domestic and international travel. Its New Jersey location makes it the natural first airport to check for most trips.

Major commercial and international airport.

Official EWR website ↗
JFK
Approx. 31 direct miles

John F. Kennedy International Airport

A major international gateway in Queens with extensive long-haul and global airline service. It can be especially useful when a desired international itinerary is unavailable or less practical from Newark.

Major commercial and international airport.

Official JFK website ↗
LGA
Approx. 29 direct miles

LaGuardia Airport

A major New York City airport focused heavily on domestic and shorter-haul passenger travel. Depending on airline, schedule and destination, LaGuardia can provide useful alternatives to Newark.

Major commercial passenger airport.

Official LGA website ↗
PHL
Approx. 67 direct miles

Philadelphia International Airport

A full-scale international airport serving the Philadelphia region. For certain routes, fares or schedules, PHL can be a worthwhile alternative to the New York metropolitan airports.

Major commercial and international airport.

Official PHL website ↗
TTN
Approx. 31 direct miles

Trenton-Mercer Airport

A smaller regional passenger airport in Mercer County. Its scale can make the airport simpler to navigate, although commercial schedules and destinations are much more limited than at EWR, JFK, LGA or PHL.

Regional passenger airport. Check current airline service directly.

Official TTN resource ↗
TEB
Approx. 25 direct miles

Teterboro Airport

A prominent New York metropolitan business and general aviation airport in northern New Jersey. Teterboro is not a conventional scheduled-airline alternative to Newark, JFK or LaGuardia.

Business, private and general aviation.

Port Authority airport information ↗
SWF
Approx. 66 direct miles

New York Stewart International Airport

Located in the lower Hudson Valley, Stewart provides another regional passenger option north of New York City. Airline routes and frequencies can vary, so current service should be checked before planning around it.

Regional commercial airport with changing route availability.

Distance & Travel Note

Use mileage as context, not as a trip-time promise.

The mileage shown on this page and map is approximate direct distance from North Edison for geographic comparison. Driving mileage can be materially longer, particularly to JFK, LaGuardia and Stewart, and actual travel time can change significantly with traffic, bridge and tunnel conditions, construction, weather, airport access patterns and the precise starting point within North Edison.

Flight schedules, airlines, terminals, parking, ground transportation and airport services can also change. Verify important travel details directly with the relevant airport, airline or transportation provider before departure.
