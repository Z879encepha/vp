Before you decide

Questions buyers and renters should ask.

  • What is included in the current association fee?
  • What rules apply to leasing, pets, parking, alterations, and outdoor spaces?
  • Are there pending assessments, litigation, insurance changes, or major projects?
  • Which school assignment applies to the specific address?
  • What inspections, permits, warranties, and disclosures are available?
  • Which services and utilities are the owner or tenant responsible for?
